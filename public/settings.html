Note on withholding tax: under Section 233 of the Income Tax Ordinance, the client withholds tax on your commission at source (12% if you're a tax filer, 24% if not) and deposits it to FBR against your NTN. That's why invoices show a "Net Amount Payable" below the gross commission — being a filer is worth roughly double the after-tax amount on every deal.
